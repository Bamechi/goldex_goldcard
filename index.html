Save Contact

Built for people who meet people.

Six screens. Every one of them is live in the web beta today.

The card

Tap. They have you.

Your card opens in a second. Save Contact drops you into their phone with photo, title, and every link labeled the way you named it.

The index

An index, not a pile.

Every column is a filter. Click Roles, tick founder, done. Rename anything, hide what you do not use, add your own columns. Your contacts are yours: staff cannot open them.

Intake

Learn who they are.

They type it or say it. Name, role, company, how to reach them: Goldex reads the note and files it. No form required.

Event Mode

Watch the room fill your index.

Live counters, the same contact table filtered to tonight, and one button to follow up with everyone who submitted today.

Import

Bring your contacts. Goldex sorts them.

Upload a CSV or your phone contacts. Names get cleaned, roles and cities get tagged, duplicates get merged, and Goldex suggests up to three columns worth adding.

Follow-up

Follow up by segment.

Email through Goldex or text from your own phone. Merge fields fill every name. A one-by-one queue keeps it personal.
