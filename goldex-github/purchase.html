Your first five minutes

  • Create an account. Onboarding takes your name, title, company, and a photo, then opens the Card Builder with everything filled in.
  • Publish the card. You get a link and a QR code that work immediately.
  • Settings: add phone, socials, booking link, and any extra fields (a second website, a press kit). Each one can be shown on any card.
  • Order NFC stickers, or write your link to a blank NTAG213 sticker with "Write to my own sticker."

Cards

One profile, many cards. Every card pulls from your profile; any value can be overridden for that card only. In Details shown, the Card layout block lets you reorder Photo, Name and role, Contact details, and Buttons, and hide the photo. Every field has a Show checkbox.

Save Contact downloads a vCard from the visible fields. On iPhone it opens straight into Contacts with photo, title, company, phone, email, and every link labeled the way you named it.

Intake: how people tell you who they are

They see one box and a Speak button. They write a sentence or say it, and Goldex shows what it understood (name, role, company, city, email, phone) before they send. "Fill in the details instead" opens the classic form. Their name has to appear somewhere; everything else is optional. The consent checkbox records permission for bulk follow-ups.

Contacts

Click any column name to filter or sort. Click any cell to edit. Tick rows and "Message selected," or filter and "Message all shown." Rename columns, edit their choices, hide, reorder, or add your own. Import from phone, VCF, or CSV: names are cleaned, roles and cities tagged, duplicates merged, and Goldex suggests up to three columns worth adding. Trip Mode lists everyone in a city grouped by role.

Events and Event Mode

Event Mode is the same contact index with the event filter already on, live counters, and a Note column. "Follow up with everyone from today" opens Messages with the segment selected.

Your data

Your contacts belong to you. Goldex staff cannot open your index; access is locked to your login at the database level. Nothing is shared between members. Export everything as CSV any time. Delete your account and every row goes with it. The assistant reads your data only to file it for you and is never trained on it.
